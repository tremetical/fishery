## TOP 5

**1. Bring Your Collection** — 3-4 wks
Legs: converts the Hall from a solved trophy case into a loadout you re-tune every session; the non-stacking aura rule makes *other people's* collections your content, and retired crests leave permanent visible gaps. Only idea that adds longevity to the spec as written rather than beside it.
Week-3 risk: needs two other humans. Bosses must scale 1-3 with the crest still dropping solo, or it dies on a quiet Tuesday.
SKUs: Aura Skin 199 R$ · Crest Wall Expansion 299 R$ · Hall Presets 249 R$ (cut if it feels like selling the walking).

**2. Fix the Rejects** — 3 wks
Legs: the only accumulator here that is literally unique per account, and it converts the 84% Common output from chute-fodder into the parts economy. Art skill multiplies instead of adds.
Week-3 risk: with 12 heads and 12 arms, "1-of-1" feels identical by week three. Rides entirely on the UNRUN cadence test — >12h per archetype and this re-scales or dies.
SKUs: Parts Bin +40 149 R$ · Paint Palettes 99 R$ · Second Bench 199 R$.

**3. Spot the Fake** — 3 wks
Legs: the accumulator is knowledge, which cannot be bought, boosted or gifted. Content unit is "draw an item, then draw it slightly wrong" — a weekly patch is one maker.
Week-3 risk: a wiki publishes the tell list. Only structural answer is forgery generations that rotate, so the wiki is dated on publication. If the negotiation reads as a dialogue tree it dies first.
SKUs: Vault Expansion 249 R$ · Second Bench 349 R$ · Shop Front Kit 199 R$. No SKU ever hints at authenticity.

**4. Make It Perfect** — 3.5 wks
Legs: asymptotic pushes plus Bee Swarm hidden Potential means a finished collection becomes unfinished the day you learn what a good roll looks like. Coprime cool timers mean there is never a clean moment to leave.
Week-3 risk: it is the least legible idea in a thumbnail and the payoff is weeks out — brutal in the sub-60s bounce bucket. Needs a scripted first push that lands huge, a published table, and a pity floor.
SKUs: Two Extra Benches 299 R$ · Forge/Anvil Skins 99 R$ · Assay Glass 149 R$. No Robux reroll, no lock token — policy names both.

**5. Open Your Factory** — 3 wks
Legs: the Visitor Wall is uncapped, unbuyable, un-alt-farmable and takes a year to look impressive; the capped Exhibit Row forces eviction where museum tycoons reward hoarding.
Week-3 risk: nobody visits, the wall stops growing, and it collapses into a solo tycoon. Public one-tap "Open Now" queue and NPC tourists must ship at launch, with a real signature worth 10x an NPC's.
SKUs: Second Shift 399 R$ · Exhibit Case +1 (caps at 12) 249 R$ · Marquee Kit 149 R$.
